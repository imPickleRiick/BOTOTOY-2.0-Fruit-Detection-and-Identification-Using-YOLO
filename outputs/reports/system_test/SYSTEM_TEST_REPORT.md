Observations from visually reviewing the annotated images (`unseen/`):

- YOLO11n names the fruit correctly in 11 of 12 photos, but on piles it often draws ONE box around the whole group (the basket of apples, the supermarket display, the orange tree), so the count is low. This is the group-box labeling style it learned from the dataset (see the Phase 4 evaluation).
- The only wrong YOLO label is the green, unripe orange (called Apple): the training oranges are almost all orange.
- The OpenCV baseline finds the right type in 7 of 12 photos but adds wrong types in most of them, because colored regions such as leaves, crates and fruit flesh match its color rules.

## Bugs found and fixed in Phase 6

- **Memory (critical):** the app cached full-resolution arrays; one 12-megapixel photo used 72 MB (baseline 112 MB) and up to 24 were cached, enough to exhaust a 6 GB laptop. The cache now keeps 1600 px previews plus the full-resolution annotated JPEG (12 MB / 18 MB per photo, at most 12 cached), batch outputs are capped at 2048 px (boxes stay in original pixels) and the batch ZIP is built once. Covered by regression tests.
