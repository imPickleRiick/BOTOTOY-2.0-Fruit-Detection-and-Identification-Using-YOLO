## 3. Quality-check false alarms (1,432 test images, not used for tuning)

The reader warned about **350 of 1,432** normal dataset photos (**24.4%**). First issue named: overexposed 180, blurry 124, motion_blur 30, dark 16. A warning never blocks the result: the reader still names the fruit it sees and adds the advice.
